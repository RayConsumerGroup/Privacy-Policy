Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and shared when you use the Your Car, Your Choice mobile application (the "App"). The App is operated by Ray Consumer Labs ("we," "us," or "our"), a subsidiary of Ray Consumer Group, which owns the underlying technology and infrastructure. Ray Consumer Labs is the entity that presents the App to the public and processes funds. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

1. Who We Are

The App is a marketplace that connects customers seeking automotive repair with independent mechanics. We facilitate introductions and payment between these parties. We are not a party to the repair agreement itself and do not perform repairs.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your email address, and a unique account identifier. Mechanics may provide additional profile details necessary to offer services.
  • Location information: we collect your device location to match customers with nearby mechanics. Location is used for matching and is not sold. You can disable location access in your device settings, though this will limit matching functionality.
  • Job and transaction information: details of repair requests, bids, prices, job status, and dispute records.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not collect or store your full card number or banking credentials; these are handled directly by Stripe under Stripe's own privacy policy. We retain records of transaction amounts, payouts, and dispute outcomes.
  • Support information: messages you send through the in-app "report a problem" feature.
  • Technical information: basic device and log data necessary to operate and secure the App.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match customers and mechanics based on location and job details.
  • To process payments, hold funds in escrow, release payouts, and resolve disputes.
  • To respond to problems you report and provide support.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and financial record-keeping obligations.

4. Payments

Payments are handled through Stripe using a hold-and-capture model: a customer's payment may be authorized and held before being captured and released to a mechanic. Dispute outcomes may result in a full release, a refund, or a split between the parties. We retain transaction and dispute records for financial and legal record-keeping. Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Planned)

We do not currently display advertising in the App. We intend to introduce advertising in the future, likely through Google AdMob. When advertising is introduced, ad providers such as Google may collect device identifiers and, subject to your consent where required, use information (which may include approximate location) to personalize ads. At that time we will update this Policy, update our App Store and Google Play privacy disclosures, and, on iOS, request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any cross-app tracking occurs. This section is a forward-looking disclosure; no advertising SDK is active in the current build.

6. How We Share Information

  • Between users: to facilitate a job, relevant details are shared between the matched customer and mechanic (for example, job location and contact necessary to complete the service).
  • Service providers: with Stripe (payments) and our backend infrastructure provider (Supabase) to operate the App.
  • Legal: where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and property.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). When you delete your account, we deactivate your login and remove or anonymize your personal profile information. However, we retain records of completed transactions, payouts, and disputes in an anonymized or pseudonymized form where possible, because we are required to keep financial records for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. Deletion may be unavailable while you have an active job or an unresolved dispute; you must resolve or complete these first.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. Account deletion is also available directly in the App.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date, and where appropriate, by notice within the App.

11. Contact
